LITTLE MANDY — WEBSITE PACKAGE

1. Upload all files and the assets folder together into /mandy/ on your website.
2. Add this HTML wherever you want the animated character:

<iframe src="/mandy/embed.html" title="Animated Mandy" style="width:100%;height:600px;border:0;background:transparent" loading="lazy"></iframe>

The background is transparent. The 48-second story smoothly transitions from a
hello to tennis, classroom learning, rowing, photography, and back. It respects the visitor's reduced-motion
preference and pauses when offscreen.

Optional: append ?activity=hello, ?activity=tennis, ?activity=teach,
?activity=rowing, or ?activity=photography to the
embed.html URL to loop a single activity. Append ?autoplay=0 to start paused.

Open index.html through your website to see the full interactive showcase.
The files must be served by a web server, not opened with a file:// URL.

mandy.glb is the portable animated 3D model. It contains the Story, Hello,
Tennis, Learning, Rowing, Photography, and Idle animation clips and the original reference face
texture. Import it into Blender or load it using a GLTFLoader, then play a clip.

The face is the original reference expression projected onto a curved 3D surface.
The hair, body, clothing, shoes, racket, ball, classroom board, boat, oars, and
camera are 3D geometry. The board contains maths, science, art/design, and
economics notes, with no programme title or slogan.
